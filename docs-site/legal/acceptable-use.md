# Acceptable use

You do not use the service to:

- collect payments for goods or services that are illegal where you or the payer are;
- attack, probe or overload the service or other projects;
- test card numbers you do not own or run card testing;
- hide the real nature of a payment, or collect money on behalf of someone else without telling us;
- send webhook or return URLs that point at internal networks;
- resell access without our written agreement.

We may suspend a project that breaks this policy or that puts the service or other users at risk.
