Take payments. Get told when they land.

Your app asks the gateway to collect an amount. The gateway offers the customer Lightning, on-chain Bitcoin, cards, bank payments or PayPal, and sends your app a signed webhook when the money has arrived.

What it is

One payment object

Create a payment once. The customer chooses how to pay, and every attempt hangs off the same payment.

Three ways to integrate

Call the API and draw your own screen, send customers to the hosted pay page, or embed it in an iframe.

Safe to retry

Idempotency keys on create, signed webhooks with three days of retries, and exactly one settlement per payment.
